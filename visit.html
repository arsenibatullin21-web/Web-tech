Store Information

The Qazaq Republic store is located on the second floor of MSW at 62 Kabanbay Batyr Avenue, Astana. The official store directory lists opening hours from 10:00 to 22:00. Check the latest information before travelling, especially if you are planning a visit on a holiday.
