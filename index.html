Clothing and accessories

The official online catalogue includes T-shirts, shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, trousers and jeans. It also lists caps, bags and children's clothing. These categories provide a starting point for exploring the range.

Online listings do not confirm availability at this particular branch. Before travelling for a specific item, ask customer service about the size, colour and current price. You can also browse the official catalogue (opens in a new tab).

Store location

The branch is on the second floor of MEGA Silk Way, at 62 Kabanbay Batyr Avenue, Astana. The official store directory lists opening hours of 10:00 to 22:00.
